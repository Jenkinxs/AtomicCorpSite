Plate 04 Colophon · contact

Colophon.

The Atomic Corporation is an early-stage software and service delivery company. It builds tools for computation, communication, and record-keeping — and then it runs them, which is the part that counts.

Work is done in-house: specification, implementation, deployment, and the unglamorous maintenance that follows. If you have a problem that fits the shape of the six systems above, write.
